Local Notes — a private, offline pastebin

A distraction-free writing space that lives entirely in your browser. Jot down text in a big textarea and keep multiple notes open in tabs — every open tab saves itself automatically, so nothing is ever lost. Star the ones that matter, close the rest, and search through everything later. There is no backend and no account: your notes are stored locally with IndexedDB and never leave your device.

Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device. Open source — view the source on GitHub.

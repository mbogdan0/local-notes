Local Notes — a private, offline pastebin

A distraction-free writing space that lives entirely in your browser. Jot down text in a big textarea, keep multiple notes open in tabs, save them with one click, and search through everything later. There is no backend and no account: your notes are stored locally with IndexedDB and never leave your device.

Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device. Open source — view the source on GitHub.
